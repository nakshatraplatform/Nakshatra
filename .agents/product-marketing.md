# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-19
**Basis:** founder's supplied backstory and naming decision; current personal
sharing/privacy contracts inspected for the landing implementation. This is
messaging context, not evidence of deployment or an independently verified persona.

## Product overview

VivIntro helps adults exploring arranged marriage create a **Vivara**: a personal
page shared through one current link, with separately approved private details.
The brand combines Vivah (marriage) and introduction, per the founder. Vivara is
inspired by Kannada `ವಿವರ`, an explanation/account in detail. Do not claim an
exclusive Kannada linguistic origin. Creating and sharing are currently free.

## Audience and jobs

Indians in India and abroad, with India/US as current intended launch markets.
The creator prepares and publishes their own Vivara. Families can help prepare
and circulate a published link through existing relatives/community contacts or
brokers. A recipient can read the public view without signup and need not create
a Vivara to request private access. No delegated family publishing is advertised.

## Problem, alternatives and differentiation

Repeatedly forwarded PDFs expose personal contact/birth/document information,
continue circulating after updates and require repeated formatting/export/sharing.
The product keeps the published version at one link and separates public from
owner-approved private access. Existing matrimonial sites, PDFs and informal
sharing networks are alternatives, not uniformly bad or universally paid.
VivIntro has no searchable profile directory and does not recommend matches.

## Voice and vocabulary

Warm, direct, culturally grounded and specific. Prefer **Vivara** for the shared
page/link; **VivIntro** for the application. Use public view, private details,
private draft, Show interest, approval, publish, renew and revoke consistently.
Introduction can describe the human act, not the product object. Avoid portfolio
and biodata as product names. PDFs may be discussed as the old sharing format.
The current implementation rolls this vocabulary out on the landing page only.

## Founder story and emotional context

Rahul's idea was to move from repeatedly forwarded PDFs to a personal page shared
by link. Marriage involves getting to know the person and family beyond a résumé
and photos. The product can begin a formal, soulful conversation; it cannot reveal
or verify someone's true self, establish compatibility or guarantee trust/outcomes.
Do not invent first-person anecdotes, founder quotes, testimonials or statistics.

## Proof and objections

Current source supports explicit draft/publication separation; a public view that
requires no account; confirmed-email sign-in and owner approval for private access;
15-day renewable/revocable grants; current photo/privacy settings and optional
horoscope upload; light/dark appearance. Do not advertise a template marketplace.
Exact field and lifecycle boundaries remain owned by privacy/domain contracts.
Screenshots/saved copies cannot be recalled. Unpublishing pauses viewing; valid
grants may resume on republish. Liveness confirms live presence, not identity,
photo ownership, profile accuracy or an absence of bots. No verified fixed
48-hour provider-deletion promise exists.

## Goals and unknowns

Primary landing action: Create your Vivara (`/signup`); secondary: sample (`/demo`).
Explain the name before using it without context. Build trust through accountable
founder context and demonstrable controls, not trust badges or absolute guarantees.
No conversion metrics, validated keyword volumes or customer proof are supplied.
Naming/trademark clearance, legal review and real-user comprehension remain open.

02 / SYMBOL EXPLORATION

Three ways to
make an introduction.

All three concepts avoid hearts, rings, couple silhouettes and traditional matrimony symbols. One direction connects most closely to the portfolio itself.

RECOMMENDED

01 — Opening chapter

A folded folio and a subtle V. The interrupted edge suggests an opening the owner chooses to make. Story and agency in one compact mark.

Tradeoff: can read as a book; the wordmark and introduction language supply category context.
ALTERNATIVE

02 — Chosen frame

An authored story inside a selectively open frame. Strong for portfolios, previews and editorial systems.

Tradeoff: closer to familiar document and scanning icons; less distinctive at small sizes.
ALTERNATIVE

03 — Shared line

Two lines meet to form a V, joined by a short bridge. A simple expression of a meaningful connection.

Tradeoff: most abstract and compact, but less specific to personal storytelling.

03 / THE SIGNATURE

Your Story. Your Introduction. Your Control.

A human wordmark

Manrope 600, fixed spacing and outlined for reliable reproduction. Capital V and I preserve the two parts of VivIntro. One weight, one color, no slogan built into the artwork.

Room to breathe

Clear space: at least one quarter of symbol width on all sides. Digital minimums: symbol 24px wide; full lockup 144px; wordmark 112px. Below 24px, use the dedicated favicon.

Quiet confidence

Use deep teal or ink on paper. Reverse to warm white on teal or slate. No gradients, shadows, stretching, extra outlines or verification badges attached to the signature.

TENOR SANS · CHAPTERS

01 — MY STORY

Scale. Hero 48–80px; section 32–48px; card 24–32px; body 16–18px; labels 12–14px. These are proposed ranges, not a global CSS reset.

Rhythm. Body line height 1.55–1.7; headlines 1.1–1.2. Keep reading columns around 55–70 characters.

Accessibility. Essential copy stays sentence case. Preserve 200% zoom, visible focus and 44px touch targets. Use native script fallbacks for multilingual content.

06 / A VISUAL LANGUAGE

A chapter system.
A deliberate opening.

Graphic motifs come from the architecture: a stable personal record, multiple views and a clear boundary between introduction and approved access.

010203

Chapter rails

Thin rules and numbers structure a narrative. Active states use text and shape as well as color.

Layered views

Offset sheets show different views of one story. Keep decorative layers out of small mobile layouts.

YOUR STORY

Open frames

One interrupted edge creates a recognizable graphic device. It must never look like a functional access status.

Icon direction: 24px grid, 1.75–2px stroke, rounded ends, restrained detail. Continue the application’s Lucide family. These sketches illustrate the style; use actual Lucide components in production.
